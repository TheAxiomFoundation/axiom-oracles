# EStG §64 recipient priority: source-bound worklist

The [review receipt](../conformance/closure/de-discovery-2026-09-08/recipient-priority-source-review.json)
retains the full pinned §64 and §78 texts and seven complete DA-KG sections:
A9, A24, A25, A25.1, A25.2, A26 and A27. A25 is a structural heading.
Their hashes match the native heading receipt. No recipient-priority module
has been generated or declared by this review.

Eligibility must be computed before priority is resolved. Section64 selects
among entitled claimants; a supplied `eligible_claimants` list or
`recipient_priority` Boolean would hide the §62/63 prerequisites. Household
membership also needs its defining rules: A9 requires shared family life,
material support and care; temporary absence can preserve membership. A25.2
excludes visits and holidays alone. Do not replace these conditions with
registration or an invented overnight cutoff.

| Branch | Observable evidence | Required computation and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Household priority | Residence and care records, actual shared-home intervals | Determine household membership and the priority among otherwise entitled claimants |
| Joint selection | Signed selection, recorded declaration, electronic application and sender confirmation | A25.1 distinguishes ordinary written consent from the electronic route; a generic consent flag misses this distinction |
| Parents and grandparents | Relationship records and waiver delivered to the Familienkasse | Parent priority remains unless the required written or recorded waiver exists; electronic application alone does not remove this form requirement |
| Selection termination | Revocation, departure and application receipt events | A25.1(5) gives following-month effects and exceptions; shared care after separation can preserve an earlier selection |
| Family-court selection | Issued decision, notified parties and dates, specified beneficiary and period, later orders | The decision must select an entitled person; FamFG40 effectiveness and stated temporal effects need their source rules |
| Maintenance priority | Actual recurring cash transfers with dates, purpose and origin | Compare ongoing cash maintenance; exclude in-kind care, occasional gifts, arrears and forwarded Kindergeld as specified in A26 |
| Equal or absent maintenance | Derived recurring amounts plus selection or court records | Preserve the selection route; an occasional lower payment does not automatically cancel an existing determination |
| December1990 transition | Payment records, residence history, child records and incoming application dates | Compose §78 with continuing eligibility and A27's full-calendar-month interruption rule; signed record helpers alone do not resolve this branch |

A26(1) sentence2 prints a reference to §64(2) sentence2 while describing the
higher-maintenance rule. The pinned statute places that rule in §64(3)
sentence2. Preserve the guidance quotation verbatim and ground the operative
maintenance comparison in the actual statutory owner.

A25.1(7) cites FamFG §40(1) for effectiveness after notification to all
participants. FamFG40 is absent from this release and requires official corpus
capture. The older BFH III R91/03, III R66/04 and VI R224/98 decisions are also
absent from its complete-decision rows. III R3/13 and III R57/13 are captured,
but their complete texts still need review before their holdings are bound in
this workstream. These source gaps and the unencoded entitlement/household
rules remain open dependencies; this worklist does not turn them into inputs.
