## Released-engine replay and computed flip

The executable manifest pins Axiom Rules Engine:

- release `v0.2.1`
- commit `dc7eb90d2cfcf6b7ecf68ada226112a8ac444350`
- target `x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu`
- archive SHA-256 `9c0fd33be8bdfb3449aa1776531e2a18cc6762372bdf427e41178616c34974ad`

On the provisioned x86_64 Linux oracle host, `scripts/de_executable.py --run`:

1. validates the pinned RuleSpec commit/tree and the EStG 66 module/apply bytes;
2. validates RuleSpec semantics, corpus binding, trusted key, and Ed25519 apply signature;
3. runs EUROMOD and GETTSIM on the canonical 13 households without a report-reemit path;
4. digest-checks, extracts, and executes the released Axiom archive;
5. binds both pair legs to identical population, requests, Axiom rows, and module/apply hashes;
6. emits the signed descriptor, pair records, unified record, replay receipt, and executable status atomically.

Ordinary receipt validation freshly extracts the embedded release archive and
recomputes its version, binary, compiled artifact, stdout, and 13 case results.
Expected replay values come only from the common Axiom execution rows. The
historical EUROMOD/GETTSIM EUR 765 aggregate is post-execution conservation
evidence, never a per-case expected-value generator.

`scripts/certify.py` computes:

`certified = conformant AND exercised AND closed AND executable AND no_open_defects`

There is no hand-edited flip.
